- https://github.com/SimoHypers/limusic

Ryotunes 2.0 was rebuilt from scratch in Go and reuses no 1.x source code. It keeps the user-facing behaviour, data paths, library, settings, account sessions and lifecycle contract that let existing installations move to the rebuilt application.
